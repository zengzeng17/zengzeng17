# zengzeng17

**AI × communication / AI × 传播**

I work where technology, stories, and people meet.

我在技术、故事和人的交叉处工作。

I am interested in how AI enters everyday life, how a public account becomes a place for ideas, and how communication can make unfamiliar technology feel closer.

我关心 AI 如何进入日常生活，官号如何成为想法发生的地方，以及传播如何让陌生的技术变得更靠近人。

## Selected work / 项目经历

### AI restores Thangka / AI 修复唐卡

把 AI 放进传统文化的修复现场。比起证明技术有多强，我更想看看它能不能让更多人愿意靠近一件古老的东西。

Bringing AI into the restoration of Thangka. I care less about proving what the technology can do than about helping more people feel close to an old piece of culture.

### AI helps cats and dogs / AI 救助猫狗

从流浪动物救助中的具体问题出发，尝试让 AI 成为一个更耐心、更实际的帮手。

Starting from the everyday problems of animal rescue, and trying to make AI a more patient and practical helper.

### Official-account communication / 官号传播

做公众号、视频号、小红书和抖音等官号内容。比起把内容发出去，我更在意一条信息如何被看见、被理解、被记住。

I work on content for official channels across WeChat, video, Xiaohongshu, and Douyin. I care about how an idea gets noticed, understood, and remembered.
